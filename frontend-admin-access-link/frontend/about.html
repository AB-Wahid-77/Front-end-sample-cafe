How We Started

One roaster, one garage, one idea.

Amber & Ash began with a single drum roaster and a simple belief: coffee tastes better when someone cares about every step, from the farm to the cup. We still roast in small batches, still cup every lot before it goes on the menu, and still close the loop with growers we've met in person.
